Privacy Policy

Last updated November 26, 2026.

Everything Stays on Your iPhone

MisogiMindset stores the areas you choose, your ratings and reflections, your Growth Wheel history and your settings only on this iPhone, in the app's private storage. There's no account, and the app has no servers.

We Collect Nothing

The app doesn't send your data anywhere. It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't share anything with anyone.

Backups

Like other app data, your MisogiMindset data is included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backups when you have them turned on. Those backups are handled by Apple and your own devices, not by MisogiMindset.

Reminders

Check-in reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Their text never includes your areas, ratings or reflections.

Your Control

Settings → Export Data gives you a copy of everything as JSON or CSV. Settings → Delete All Data permanently erases everything from the app. Deleting the app also deletes its data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app? Email misogimindset@gmail.com.
