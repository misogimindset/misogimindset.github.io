Support & Feedback

MisogiMindset helps you grow in the areas of life that matter to you. You rate each area on a Growth Wheel, check in with a quick daily rating, and reflect with short prompts.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up, and everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

How do I keep a copy of my data?

Settings → Export Data shares everything as JSON (a full backup) or CSV (for a spreadsheet). Your data is also included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backups, if you have them turned on.

How do I start over?

Settings → Delete All Data permanently erases everything and starts setup again. Export first if you want a copy.

How do I change my reminder?

Settings → Reminders sets how often (daily, weekly or off) and what time. If reminders don't appear, check that notifications are allowed for MisogiMindset in the iPhone's Settings app.

How many areas can I have?

Up to 8 at once, including up to 4 of your own. Removing an area keeps its history, and adding it back restores it.

Is MisogiMindset a substitute for professional help?

No. It's a personal growth tool, not a medical or therapy service.
